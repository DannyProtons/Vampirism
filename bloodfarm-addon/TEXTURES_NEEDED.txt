TEXTURES NEEDED FOR BLOOD FARM MOD
====================================

This mod requires custom 16x16 pixel textures. Place them in the following locations:

ITEM TEXTURE:
-------------
src/main/resources/assets/bloodfarm/textures/item/blood_fruit.png
  - A dark red/crimson fruit with a blood-like appearance
  - Suggested style: Similar to a sweet berry but darker, with a glossy/wet look

CROP STAGE TEXTURES (8 total):
-------------------------------
All in: src/main/resources/assets/bloodfarm/textures/block/

blood_crop_stage0.png - Tiny sprout, barely visible
blood_crop_stage1.png - Small dark stem emerging
blood_crop_stage2.png - Growing stem with first dark leaves
blood_crop_stage3.png - Larger plant, darker red coloring
blood_crop_stage4.png - Maturing plant, small buds visible
blood_crop_stage5.png - Buds growing, crimson color intensifying
blood_crop_stage6.png - Nearly mature, fruits forming
blood_crop_stage7.png - Fully grown with ripe blood fruits

STYLE SUGGESTIONS:
------------------
- Dark, vampiric aesthetic
- Deep reds, crimsons, and dark purples
- Gothic/horror theme to match Vampirism mod
- Consider adding dripping blood effect on mature stages
- Dark green/black stems and leaves

TEMPORARY SOLUTION:
-------------------
Until you create custom textures, you can use placeholder textures from vanilla:
- Copy wheat crop textures and tint them red as a temporary solution
- The mod will work but textures will be missing/purple-black checkerboard
