# How to Obtain Blood Fruit - Acquisition Guide

Blood Fruit is the seed item for Blood Crops. This guide explains all the ways to obtain your first seeds.

## 🌱 Method 1: Crafting (Most Common)
**Once you have at least one Blood Fruit, you can keep farming it!**

### Recipe: Shapeless Crafting
```
Blood Fruit → Blood Fruit (1:1)
```

Simply place a Blood Fruit in any crafting grid (inventory works!) to get it back as a seed. This allows you to:
- Convert harvested fruits into seeds
- Share seeds with other players
- Always have seeds available

**Best Practice:** When harvesting, always keep 1-2 fruits as seeds for replanting!

---

## 🏚️ Method 2: Vampire Hut Chests (Easy Start)
**Find vampire huts in the Vampire Forest biome**

- **Location**: Vampire Forest biome
- **Chest Loot**: 15% chance
- **Amount**: 1-3 Blood Fruits per chest
- **Difficulty**: Easy (small structures, usually unguarded)

**Tips:**
- Explore Vampire Forest biomes
- Look for small wooden huts with cursed atmosphere
- Check all chests in the hut

---

## ⛪ Method 3: Vampire Altar Chests (Better Rewards)
**Raid vampire altars for better loot**

- **Location**: Vampire Altar structures (various biomes)
- **Chest Loot**: 25% chance (higher than huts!)
- **Amount**: 2-5 Blood Fruits per chest
- **Difficulty**: Medium (larger structure, possibly guarded by vampires)

**Tips:**
- Altars are rarer but have better loot
- Bring weapons - vampires may spawn nearby
- Multiple chests mean multiple chances

---

## 🧛 Method 4: Advanced Vampire Drops (Late Game)
**Kill Advanced Vampires for rare drops**

- **Drop Source**: Advanced Vampire mobs
- **Drop Chance**: ~2% (rare!)
- **Amount**: 1-2 Blood Fruits
- **Looting Effect**: +0-1 per Looting level
- **Difficulty**: Hard (dangerous mobs)

**Tips:**
- Not reliable for starting out
- Good for occasional bonus seeds
- Looting III increases drops significantly
- Best done with vampire-hunting gear

## 🎯 Recommended Strategy

### For New Players:
1. **Explore Vampire Forest** to find a Vampire Hut
2. **Loot chests** to get your first 1-3 Blood Fruits
3. **Plant immediately** and wait for harvest
4. **Use crafting** to convert fruits to seeds as needed
5. **Expand farm** gradually

### For Mid-Game Players:
1. **Raid Vampire Altars** for 2-5 fruits (better yield)
2. **Set up large farm** (9×9 around water)
3. **Harvest regularly** and keep some as seeds

### For Late-Game Players:
1. **Automate farming** with hoppers/droppers
2. **Mass production** for blood grinder operations
3. **Vampire hunting** provides occasional bonus seeds

## 🧪 Integration with Vampirism

Blood Farm integrates seamlessly with Vampirism's:
- **Structures**: Huts and Altars now contain Blood Fruit
- **Mobs**: Advanced Vampires have rare drops
- **Loot System**: Uses Vampirism's injection system

All loot generation is done through data files, so:
- ✅ Compatible with other mods
- ✅ Configurable via datapacks
- ✅ No conflicts with Vampirism updates

---

## 🔧 Technical Details

### Loot Table Weights:
- Vampire Hut: 15 weight (Blood Fruit) vs 85 weight (nothing) = 15% chance
- Vampire Altar: 25 weight vs 75 weight = 25% chance
- Advanced Vampire: 2 weight vs 98 weight = 2% chance

### Loot Injection:
Files are placed in `data/vampirism/loot_table/inject/` to add to existing Vampirism loot tables without replacing them. This means:
- Other mods can also add to the same tables
- Vampirism updates won't break the integration
- Fully modular and removable

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Can I trade with vampire villagers for Blood Fruit?**
A: Not in the current version, but this may be added in future updates!

**Q: Does Blood Fruit spawn naturally in the world?**
A: No, only in structure chests and mob drops. This keeps it balanced.

**Q: Can I use bone meal on Blood Crops?**
A: Yes! Bone meal works to speed up growth.

**Q: What if I can't find Vampire Forest?**
A: Use `/locate biome vampirism:vampire_forest` (requires cheats) or explore thoroughly. They're somewhat rare.

**Q: I lost all my Blood Fruit! What now?**
A: Find another Vampire Hut or Altar chest, or hunt Advanced Vampires. Once you have even one fruit, you can rebuild your farm.
